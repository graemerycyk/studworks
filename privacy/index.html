Your projects and your privacy

What goes where.

No builder account. A clear choice before sharing.

Effective 7 September 2026. The online workbench, cloud connections and community submissions are not yet publicly available. Their sections below explain how those optional features are designed to handle information when enabled.

Who to contact

Studworks is provided under the name studworks.build, based in Belgium. For privacy questions and requests, contact help@studworks.build.

The current website and support

The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes connection and usage information, such as IP addresses and request times, under its privacy statement. Studworks does not add advertising or analytics tracking.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send so we can respond. Do not send passwords, access tokens or private project details that are not needed to explain your question.

The native app

The normal Studworks app workflow runs on your device. Your instructions, saved machine details, project versions and run history stay there unless you choose an online connection, export a file, send a report or submit a build. Initial app, model and hub-firmware downloads may need the internet.

The web workbench

When autosave is enabled, project copies and unfinished instructions are stored in this browser on this device. They are not a cloud project library. You can pause autosave and remove individual copies in My projects. Pausing does not erase existing copies. Clearing browser data removes them; download important projects as a backup. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to open them.

Making, checking or revising a draft sends the relevant project text to the Studworks service. Opening a saved plan for checking sends that plan too. File inspection sends the file you explicitly ask us to inspect. Preparing a guarded program also sends selected hub-check evidence and your chosen device roles. These planning requests are processed temporarily rather than saved in a private server-side project library.

Autosave never stores a live Bluetooth connection, authentication token or run approval. Authorizing an external connection does not grant permission for a physical run.

Claude, ChatGPT and other tools

If you connect another AI service, it can receive the project and machine information covered by the access you allow. The online bridge temporarily relays proposals and results. Current AI-session permission lasts up to 30 minutes and can be ended locally. Each physical run still needs a separate local approval.

Your chosen provider has its own account, pricing, retention and privacy rules. The native app’s offline promise does not cover information you choose to send to that provider.

Submitting a build

Sharing is optional. Submissions contain the title, description, hardware, instruction, photo or video link, permissions and any project file you choose to attach. You may choose anonymous credit or a name or username and an optional public blog or social link. That credit is content on the submission, not an account or a verified identity.

Submissions are stored privately for moderation. Only approved builds appear publicly. Attached project files can contain notes and earlier versions, so review them first. Do not include private contact details, location information or images of people without appropriate permission. Links take visitors to another website; Studworks does not control that website’s privacy practices.

Keep your private receipt: it lets you check the submission and withdraw it. Withdrawal removes the submitted content and attachment from the live gallery and database, leaving a minimal administrative record temporarily. It cannot remove copies already downloaded, search-engine caches or the original media on another site. With no builder email collected, a lost receipt cannot simply be emailed back.

Retention and service providers

The proposed online service uses DigitalOcean hosting and PostgreSQL for submissions and moderator access. Moderator sign-in emails use Resend. Hosting and mail providers necessarily handle connection or delivery information. The operator must confirm production regions, contractual safeguards, international transfers, access-log settings and backup retention before launch.

The implemented cleanup policy removes pending submission content after 90 days and rejected content after 30 days, then removes withdrawn records after a further 30 days. Moderation events expire after 365 days or when their associated withdrawn record is removed. Published builds remain until withdrawal. These deletions depend on a scheduled cleanup job; its operation and backup deletion schedule still require deployment verification.

Cookies, diagnostics and training

Studworks does not add advertising or analytics tracking. Essential cookies support browser authorization and moderator login. Moderator sessions expire after eight hours; sign-in links expire after five minutes. The workbench keeps an autosave preference and, when enabled, local project copies.

The debug-report preview excludes instruction text, program output, project notes, names and credentials by default. Nothing is sent automatically. The separate raw console or request-copy tools can include private text; check it before sharing. Community publication does not automatically add a build to model training; any training permission is separate.

Your choices and requests

You can remove local copies, end an online connection and use your receipt to withdraw a submission. Contact the address above to ask about access, correction, deletion or other data-protection rights that apply to you. We may need enough information to locate the record and verify your authority without collecting unnecessary details.

Where applicable, your rights include access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection and withdrawal of consent. You may also contact the Belgian Data Protection Authority or your local supervisory authority. A parent, guardian or teacher should handle public submissions for children. Production processing details and young-builder arrangements will be reviewed before online submissions open.
