Studworks

Tell a LEGO machine what to do. It works out the rest.

Connect a Powered Up hub. Studworks finds out what is plugged in, asks about anything it cannot see, turns what you typed into a program, compiles it on your own device and puts it on the hub. Then you tell it what to change.

Why it is different

Most tools that write code for you do not know what is on the other end of the cable. Studworks starts by asking the hub. It knows which motor is your barrier and which is your train, and when it does not know, it asks rather than guessing — because moving the wrong motor is the one mistake worth avoiding above all others.

  • It works offline. No account, no API key, no server. Nothing you type leaves your device.
  • It is free. Not a trial.
  • Every program is bounded. Motors coast when a program ends, moves have deadlines, and a jam is abandoned rather than pushed through.
  • You correct it in words. "Too slow", "stay down longer", "not so far".
