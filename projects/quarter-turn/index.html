02 / First movement

One small turn.

Before the crane, conveyor or crossing: get one loose motor doing one thing.

By Grae · Development-tested

This experiment moves a motor. Use an unloaded motor, with no mechanism attached. Secure it, keep the shaft clear, and keep both the app’s Stop control and the hub’s physical button within reach. Guardrails reduce risk; they do not remove it.

On the workbench

Hub
City Hub 88009
Motor
Technic Large Motor 88013 on A
Firmware tested
Pybricks 4.0.1

This action was tried on an earlier development build. The current release still needs its exact-build hardware checks. These notes are not qualification for another motor or an attached mechanism.

Before making it move

  1. Connect the hub in Studworks and complete Self-test (no motion). If it fails, do not continue.
  2. Choose What’s plugged in? and check that the detected device on A is your Technic Large Motor.
  3. Confirm that this is the intended test motor, that it is unloaded and that it has room to turn. Passing self-test is not permission to move it.

What to tell Studworks

Turn the motor 90 degrees slowly
  1. Press Make it. If Studworks asks which motor you mean, confirm the physically checked motor on A.
  2. Review the result. In our earlier test it requested one 90-degree turn at 200 degrees per second. Stop if the selected motor or action is not what you intended.
  3. Choose Run it once and watch the motor. It should make one bounded turn and finish. If it behaves unexpectedly, use Stop; if stopping is not effective, use the hub’s physical button.
  1. Confirm motor
  2. Turn 90°
  3. Finish and coast

A quarter-turn of what?

The instruction describes the motor shaft, not an attached crane or barrier. Gearing changes the relationship, and a mechanism may have hard stops. Do not reuse this movement on a construction without understanding its permitted travel.

Each fresh run asks for another relative turn. Running it again does not return it to its starting position.

Evidence: Grae confirmed this quarter-turn worked on the development fixture. A completed console message alone does not prove physical movement or position. New release builds must be checked again.
