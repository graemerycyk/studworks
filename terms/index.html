The product

Studworks is provided under the name studworks.build, based in Belgium. Contact help@studworks.build.

Studworks helps you describe, inspect and program compatible LEGO machines. It is free, forever. Not a trial. There is no Studworks builder account or subscription. Hardware, internet access and external AI services are separate and may have their own costs and terms.

The beta supports the capabilities identified in the app and documentation. It does not promise every Pybricks feature or every LEGO device. Online features may be interrupted for maintenance, faults or abuse prevention. Keep your own exported project backups.

Physical actions need your review

Use a suitable, attended test setup. Check which motor or sensor does what, keep the hub’s physical stop button accessible and review each proposed movement. Connecting a chat tool, opening a file or sharing a project does not authorize movement. Do not bypass safeguards or use Studworks for safety-critical equipment.

Software completion does not prove a mechanism reached the right physical position. A lost connection or failed stop can leave the physical outcome uncertain. Stop and inspect the machine before deciding what to do next. Motors may coast; holding torque is not promised.

Only the limited improvement records described in the privacy notice are in scope; whole project files, private notes, imported source and unrelated session content are not automatically included.

Reviewing examples and training a model do not automatically validate an interpretation or authorize a physical action. A completed program alone does not prove that Studworks understood the request. Your applicable data-protection rights are not waived by these terms.

Choosing to share

Submit only work and links you have permission to share. By explicitly submitting for publication, you give permission limited to storing, reviewing and displaying that submitted content and the credit you selected. If you attach a portable project for others to build from, the form must clearly explain the permission for downloading and adapting it before that option is publicly launched.

Submissions are reviewed before publication. We may decline or remove unlawful, unsafe, misleading, abusive or privacy-invasive material. Publication is not an endorsement or a hardware qualification. Anonymous credit is allowed; a name or username is self-selected and unverified.

Your private receipt lets you withdraw a submission. Withdrawal cannot recall copies already shared or downloaded. Permission to publish a community build is limited to that sharing purpose; it does not enroll the full submitted project, attachments or media into the service-improvement dataset.

Responsible use

Do not attempt to control another person’s hub, obtain their credentials, evade run approval or overload the service. Do not post someone’s private information. For a young builder, a parent, guardian or teacher should handle public sharing until the launch age and consent arrangements have been confirmed.
